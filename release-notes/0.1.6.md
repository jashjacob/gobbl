# Gobbl 0.1.6

Gob now works along with OpenCode too.

- Connect OpenCode in Settings → Agents: Gob follows its sessions live, with the coding and thinking faces and a cheer when a task finishes
- Gobbl adds only its own plugin file, ~/.config/opencode/plugin/gobbl.js, and removes it again when you disconnect
- Allow and Deny from the notch stay Claude Code's; OpenCode, Codex and Grok show status and celebrations
