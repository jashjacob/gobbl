Your notch has a pet. The first release of Gobbl.
- Hatch your own pet from an egg: 8 species, 1 in 100 hatch shiny.
- Drop files on the notch and Gob keeps them on the shelf until you drag them out. AirDrop, share, Quick Look.
- Right-click a shelved file to convert or compress images, PDFs and videos, remove a photo's background, copy its text, or zip it. All on your Mac.
- Clipboard history with search and pins, on ⇧⌘Space. Passwords are never saved.
- Music from any app in the notch, with artwork and controls. Gob dances along.
- Volume, brightness and Caps Lock in the notch instead of the system popups (optional).
- Your next meeting with a Join button, and a nudge five minutes before.
- A focus timer and keep-awake, one click away.
- Share your pet's card.
