Your notch has a pet.

Gobbl moves a tiny creature into your MacBook's notch. It gobbles the files you drop, keeps your clipboard, dances to your music and nudges you before meetings.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Gobbl is MIT-licensed and open source, by Xeve. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Does it drain my battery?

No. At rest Gobbl uses about 0% CPU: Gob only redraws to blink, and nothing polls in the background except a cheap clipboard check.

My Mac doesn't have a notch.

Gobbl shows a small pill at the top of the screen instead, on any display you like.

What does it collect?

Nothing. Clipboard history and the shelf live only on your Mac. Permissions (Accessibility, Calendar) are optional and asked only when you turn a feature on.

Can I make my own pet?

Custom skins are coming. Star the repo to follow along.
