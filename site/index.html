Questions

    
Is it really free?
Yes. Gobbl is MIT-licensed and open source, by Xeve. No account, no subscription, no ads.

    
When can I download it?
The first signed, notarized release is coming very soon. Star the repo to get notified, or build it yourself from the source today.

    
Does it drain my battery?
No. At rest Gobbl sits around 0% CPU: Gob only redraws to blink, and nothing polls in the background except a cheap clipboard check.

    
My Mac doesn't have a notch.
Gobbl shows a small pill at the top of the screen instead, on any display you like.

    
How does the Claude Code link work?
Clicking Connect adds a small hook to Claude Code's settings (backed up first). Events go to Gobbl over a private socket on your Mac. If Gobbl isn't running or you don't answer a prompt within 30 seconds, Claude simply asks in the terminal as usual.
